CCA Música

Individual Website Project — Site Plan

Site Name

CCA Música

I selected this name because it represents the music academy where I currently study singing. It reflects the academy's identity and commitment to helping students develop their musical talents and achieve their dreams.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to improve the academy's online visibility, strengthen its digital marketing, and attract prospective students.

The website will provide information about music courses, instructors, and teaching methods. It will also allow prospective students to request and schedule their first free introductory class online.

Scenarios

  • How can I start developing my musical talents and pursuing my dreams?
  • How does the music academy work, and what courses are available?
  • How can I schedule my first free music class online?
